# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individual app developers and marketers preparing preview videos for App Store and Google Play listings.

## Product Purpose

AppVid creates app-store preview videos from mobile screen recordings. Success means a user can import, edit, preview, and export a store-ready video without leaving the browser.

## Positioning

Video processing runs locally in the browser: screen recordings are not uploaded and users do not need an account.

## Operating Context

Users work in a desktop browser with mobile screen recordings, selecting a store preset, arranging video, audio, and text on a timeline, then exporting a finished preview.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Import and relink mobile video files.
- Edit media and text cues on a timeline.
- Export App Store and Google Play-ready video presets.
- Processing is local and browser-based; no uploads or accounts are required.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: AppVid.
- Preserve the local-processing and privacy promise in user-facing UI.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product editor illustration: `public/images/appvid-feature.png`.
- Existing local-first product copy and working React implementation.
- No customer testimonials, benchmarks, or external proof assets; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make store-preview production legible and controllable.
- Keep media private by default.
- Prioritize the working editor over marketing ornament.
- Preserve accessible, keyboard-operable workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, dialog focus management, and reduced-motion support.
